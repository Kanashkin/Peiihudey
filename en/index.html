How it works

Four steps from plate to number

No manual weighing or spreadsheets — the app counts, you just eat.

1

Take a photo

Of your food — for calories, or of yourself — for your personal target and weekly plan.

2

AI does the math

It identifies the food and portion size, and works out your calorie and protein target from your profile using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula.

3

Log what you ate

From your weekly plan, by voice, from a database of 300+ foods — or repeat yesterday with one tap.

4

See your progress

How much is left today, your streak of days within limit, weight over time — all at a glance on the home screen.

Where the numbers come from

Your target is calculated from your data, not an average

Based on the Mifflin-St Jeor formula for basal metabolic rate, multiplied by an activity factor.

Men
10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age + 5
Women
10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age − 161
The result is multiplied by an activity factor from ×1.2 (sedentary) to ×1.9 (extreme activity) — that gives your daily maintenance calories, from which the deficit for weight loss is calculated.
